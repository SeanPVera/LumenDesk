# LumenDesk — Light in place

LumenDesk is a local control surface for a physical room. Its distinguishing
material is the light being controlled, not a branded gradient or an imitation
lighting desk. A room is the persistent context for Light, Compositions and Music.

## Visual thesis

Named emitters occupy a continuous dark field. Their color and relative level
come from fixture state; an unlit outline remains when output is off or unknown.
Saved positions are relative placements, never calibrated architectural scale.
Where positions are unknown or too crowded, an ordered arrangement says so.
Names and state sit on opaque ground. Selection has a checkmark and boundary.
Offline has words. No essential state depends on color perception.

Darkness gives actual lighting colors space. Most interface surfaces are neutral;
color belongs to emitter marks, segment cells, palette relationships and scene
scores. It does not tint every panel. A scene score represents its stored
fixtures and levels. Music separates input, interpretation and generated output;
its preview does not claim physical device confirmation.

Precision and atmosphere share the workspace: a field describes the installation,
a shared editor changes the selected targets, and a single fixture opens its
precise color/white and spatial controls in place. Technical metadata belongs in
configuration or diagnostics. No fake knobs, engraved scales, brushed metal,
icon-only primary rail, giant hero, decorative visualizer or nested card grid.

## Voice and hierarchy

Use room, fixture and scene names as ordinary language. Say what an action will
affect. Distinguish Stop & restore from Keep current light. Say “generated” when
output is generated and “not responding” when connectivity is uncertain. State
limitations where they change a decision: browser whole-fixture output, limited
simultaneous zones, held layouts, permissions and failed placement.

Use system typography, sentence case, 12-point minimum token text and tabular
numbers. Monospace is for addresses and diagnostics. Compactness comes from
shared controls and disclosures, not microscopic text.

## Platform behavior

macOS retains windows, menus, shortcuts, undo, native color picker and focused
sheets. Named horizontal navigation replaces the old icon rail. iOS keeps native
tabs and navigation. Settings, scheduling and device administration are secondary
to the room workspace. The production browser shares that organization within
its existing bridge capabilities; it does not pretend to expose native segments.

The existing app mark remains unchanged: this redesign changes the operating
application rather than creating an unrelated logo campaign. Its geometric room
mark is compatible with the room-first direction. Token implementation and
exceptions are documented in DESIGN_SYSTEM.md; evidence and limits are in
REDESIGN_REPORT.md.
